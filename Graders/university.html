🎓 University Grading System

The 5-point scale used by Nigerian universities to calculate GPA and CGPA.

Score (%) Grade Grade Point Remark
70 – 100A5.0Excellent
60 – 69B4.0Very Good
50 – 59C3.0Good
45 – 49D2.0Fair
40 – 44E1.0Pass
0 – 39F0.0Fail
💡 Formula: GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Units, where Quality Points = Grade Point Credit Unit of each course.
